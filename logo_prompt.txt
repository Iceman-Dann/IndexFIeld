IndexField Logo Design Prompt

Create a modern, industrial logo for IndexField - an AI-powered industrial intelligence platform.

Brand Colors:
- Primary: Safety Orange (#F97316) - vibrant, energetic
- Secondary: Burnt Orange (#EA580C) - darker, professional
- Background: Charcoal (#111827) - dark, sophisticated
- Text: Off-White (#F8FAFC) - clean, readable

Design Elements:
- Typography: Clean, modern sans-serif (Inter font family)
- Style: Minimalist, professional, tech-forward
- Industry: Industrial/maintenance/operations focus
- Vibe: Sophisticated, reliable, cutting-edge AI

Logo Concepts:
1. **Abstract Field/Grid**: Geometric pattern representing data fields or industrial grid systems
2. **Index Symbol**: Stylized "I" or "IF" with data visualization elements
3. **Industrial Tech**: Fusion of gear/cog shapes with digital circuit patterns
4. **Data Flow**: Lines or nodes suggesting information processing and analysis

Technical Requirements:
- Scalable for web and mobile
- Works well on dark backgrounds
- Single color and full color versions
- Professional enough for enterprise clients
- Modern enough for tech startup appeal

Avoid:
- Overly complex designs
- Generic tech clichés (clouds, random circuit patterns)
- Playful or casual elements
- Traditional industrial imagery (hammers, wrenches)

Target Audience:
- Industrial maintenance teams
- Operations managers
- Engineering professionals
- Enterprise decision makers

Keywords: Industrial AI, data intelligence, predictive maintenance, operations optimization, smart manufacturing
